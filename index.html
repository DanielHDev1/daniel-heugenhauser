YetiFarm

YetiFarm is a student project developed for a company called YetiCare. The game includes multiple simple minigames designed for the Yeti tablet, a large touchscreen device powered by Android and aimed at people with special needs. The project focuses on creating engaging and accessible gameplay experiences that also support the development of the players cognitive, motor, and everyday skills.

  • Role: Code/Design
  • Tools: Unity, C#
  • Focus: Developing the whack-a-mole minigame
  • Status: Published
